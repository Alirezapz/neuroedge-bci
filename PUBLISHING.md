# Publication scope

The project owner has authorized publishing the source, trained model
artifacts, actual predictions and research reports on GitHub, and making the
repository public under the name `neuroedge-bci`.

The publication preserves the completed version 0.5.0 experiment bytes. Code,
method descriptions, aggregate tables and comparison figures can be inspected
directly. Split ZIP archives contain all remaining checkpoints, scalers,
prediction tables and audit records. A standard-library restoration command
verifies every archive part and restored file before use.

Four recorded-signal replay pages and the earlier individual heatmap image
are excluded from this publication. Raw EDF/MATLAB recordings, processed EEG
caches, credentials, environments and browser binaries are excluded. Exact
public download sources and hashes are retained for local reconstruction.

The source license is MIT, copyright 2026 Alireza Pazouki and NeuroEdge
contributors. Dataset terms remain separate; see DATA_LICENSES.md and
THIRD_PARTY.md. Publishing artifacts does not expand the project's rights in
third-party content or turn the studies into an independent confirmation.

See ARTIFACTS.md for retrieval, restoration and integrity checks.
